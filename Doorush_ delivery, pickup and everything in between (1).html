Questions

Good to know.

When do you launch?

Very soon, starting in Sunyani. Waitlist members hear first.

How do I pay?

At launch you pay on delivery. Card and mobile money payments through Paystack are planned next.

What can I order?

Food, pickups (including laundry), gas refills and cosmetics.

Is it free for merchants and riders?

Yes, joining is free while we launch.

How are riders verified?

Riders submit ID and bike details, and documents are reviewed within 48 hours.
